## 4. Proprietary IP: The 5-Dimension Evaluation Framework
CMT Systems audits all commercial cognitive-motor systems across five non-negotiable benchmarks:
1. **Cognitive Complexity:** High-level executive function (Stroop effect, Flanker tests, working memory, mathematical processing) vs simple light-flash reaction.
2. **Stimulus Flexibility:** Dynamic alphanumeric, spatial, color, and acoustic triggers that adapt dynamically.
3. **Response Flexibility:** Diverse motor decision choices requiring rapid neuromuscular calculation.
4. **Physical Movement (Kinetic Load):** Real biomechanical exertion (medicine balls up to 12 lbs, fists, feet, bats, rackets) vs delicate tap-only touchscreens.
5. **Performance Data & Clinical EMR Integration:** Sub-millisecond dual-task cost (DTC) latency tracking, objective progress documentation for physical therapy billing codes (CPT 97530 Therapeutic Activities, 97112 Neuromuscular Re-education).
