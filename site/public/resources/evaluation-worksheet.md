## How to Use This Worksheet

1. **Bring this worksheet to vendor demonstrations** and fill it out as you observe the equipment in action.
2. **Score each dimension (1-5)** based on documented capabilities, not marketing claims.
3. **Record the evidence source** for each score (demonstration, documentation, research).
4. **Calculate the provisional average** (only include scored dimensions).
5. **Use the summary section** to identify essential capabilities, limitations, and outstanding questions.
6. **Do not use the average as an automatic winner-selection score.** Consider which dimensions matter most for your specific application.
