# SMARTfit source library

This folder contains organized copies of PDFs Robert confirms came from SMARTfit, together with extracted sales-tool content Robert confirms came from SMARTfit's Google Drive sales tools. The PDFs were located across the prior project and its archived source materials; original files were left in place. This provenance records how Robert received the materials; it does not mean SMARTfit authored every paper or independently validates every statement within a sales tool.

## Folder map

- `product-specifications/current/2026-07/` — newest-dated grouping in the supplied project files. Document footers do not all match the folder label: the reviewed PDFs include May 2026, 2024, 2023, and February 2025 revision dates. Verify the exact sheet's currentness before publishing or quoting details.
- `product-specifications/archive/2026-03/` and `archive/2025-08/` — dated earlier specification sheets, retained for version history. Do not use as current specifications without confirmation.
- `research-and-white-papers/` — research and white papers from the source collections. Check each paper's author, sponsor, study design, population, and limitations before using it to support a claim.
- `external-background-research-not-smartfit-validation/` — related research that does not validate SMARTfit product efficacy. One paper uses SMARTfit as a real-world comparator in a Rezzil measurement study, and one conference abstract reports acute exercise sessions performed on SMARTfit; neither establishes SMARTfit training efficacy. See the document-by-document appraisal before reuse.
- `sample-reports-review-before-use/` — examples of product reports. Treat as illustrations of report format, not evidence of clinical outcomes. Confirm that any personal or health information is removed before sharing.
- `product-guides-review-before-use/` — guides whose intended audience and current status need review.
- `product-guides-review-before-use/SMARTfit-BrainHealth.pdf` — SMARTfit-originated 2025 Rev 1.3, 42-page Brain Health lead magnet, identified by Robert, who confirms permission to offer it and that its demo access is intended for prospects. Keep the PDF intact so readers can use the demo details. See [the internal review](../../strategy/smartfit-brain-health-lead-magnet-review.md).
- `service-and-warranty-review-before-use/` — internal SMARTfit service-plan material. `Platinum Service Plan 1-01-24.pdf` is included with page-marked extracted text; its terms, fees, and date/currentness require confirmation before use. - `product-manuals-internal/` — operating material for internal product reference; do not republish without permission.
- `restricted/technical-and-compliance/` — technical, certification, cloud, and tax-related files. Internal reference only until the specific document, scope, validity, and public-use permission are reviewed.
- `restricted/commercial-pricing/` — confidential US reseller pricing supplied by Robert. The July 1, 2026 price list and an internal structured extraction are excluded from Git by the project `.gitignore`; do not copy wholesale prices into the general workbook or public materials. - `source-index.csv` — source path, source set, organized destination, SHA-256 checksum, and copy/deduplication action for every PDF source entry inventoried.

Product details should be checked for currentness; research, clinical, financial, and other outcome claims are not independently substantiated or approved public copy. Its filename and PDF metadata do not establish its effective date; see [the internal review](../../strategy/platinum-service-plan-review.md) for verified text and remaining currentness questions.

## Evidence and publishing rules

1. Robert confirms the PDF collection came from SMARTfit and SMARTfit-specific marketing content came from its Google Drive sales tools. Retain this as user-reported provenance; check document authorship, version, date, and scope individually.
2. Keep these files as source material; manufacturer-originated sales language can document how SMARTfit presents its products, but does not by itself prove a clinical, research, reimbursement, financial, or comparative claim.
3. Before drafting public content from a PDF, record the exact supporting passage and page number, the document version/date, its author or issuer, and any limitations.
4. If documents disagree, preserve and flag the conflict. Do not infer which figure is correct.
5. Do not publish confidential pricing or restricted technical/compliance documents. Pricing was not found in the three inventoried PDF collections; keep pricing data in its separate private location.
6. Do not convert research findings into product efficacy, reimbursement, certification, or compliance claims unless the exact claim is supported, appropriate to the evidence, and approved for publication.
7. Exact byte-for-byte duplicates were copied once. All discovered source paths remain recorded in the index, with checksums for verification.
8. Treat research papers as separate evidence documents. Verify authorship, sponsorship, study design, and SMARTfit-product relevance regardless of where Robert obtained the PDF.
9. Keep user-provided transcriptions distinct from files actually present and extracted in the library. Record missing page references and verify the original before treating transcribed terms as exact.

The `2026-07` directory means the newest dated specification set found in this inventory, not a guarantee that SMARTfit has not since revised it.
