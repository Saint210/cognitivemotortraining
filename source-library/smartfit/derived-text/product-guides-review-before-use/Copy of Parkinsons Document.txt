SMARTﬁt: The Future of Dual-Task Training
SMARTﬁt is an innovative, evidence-based technology that revolutionizes the way 
we approach Parkinson’s disease treatment by simultaneously training the brain 
and body. This dual-tasking approach, rooted in over 30 years of scientiﬁc research, 
has been proven to deliver faster and more eﬀective results than traditional 
single-task methods.
Why SMARTﬁt?
SMARTﬁt’s gamiﬁed dual-task training is uniquely designed to enhance cognitive function, physical mobility, and 
dual-task capabilities. For individuals with Parkinson’s, this approach is not just beneﬁcial—it’s transformative. Here’s 
how SMARTﬁt makes a diﬀerence
- Physical Beneﬁts: Improve cardiovascular health, strength, balance, ﬂexibility, and 
endurance. SMARTﬁt’s engaging exercises also enhance core strength, functional 
movement, and range of motion, critical for maintaining physical independence.
- Neural and Cognitive Advantages: Boost proprioception, vestibular control, and 
hand-foot-eye coordination. SMARTﬁt’s dual-tasking regimen promotes neuroplasticity, 
cognitive agility, and motor coordination while enhancing attention, memory, and 
problem-solving skills. This comprehensive training supports the repair of neural systems, 
improves impulse control, and strengthens executive functions.
- Social and Behavioral Impact: SMARTﬁt isn’t just about physical and cognitive gains; it fosters social interaction, team 
building, and cooperation. Participants experience greater conﬁdence, self-esteem, and enjoyment, all while setting 
and achieving personal goals.
SMARTﬁt for Parkinson’s Disease: Proven Success
Parkinson’s disease, a cognitive-motor disorder, is particularly well-suited for SMARTﬁt’s dual-task training. In various 
programs, including those at the South Collier Marco Island YMCA, participants have seen remarkable improvements. A 
study led by renowned researcher Dr. Beth Fisher at the University of Southern California demonstrated that over 87% 
of participants achieved better outcomes with SmartFit compared to USC’s traditional single-task physical therapy 
program.
Transforming Daily Life
One participant remarked, “I feel the fog has lifted! I can cook again because in the 
past I couldn’t follow the recipe.” While SMARTﬁt doesn’t directly train individuals to 
cook, its dual-tasking approach enhances the brain and body’s eﬃciency, translating
into improved performance in everyday cognitive-motor activities.
Experience the SmartFit Diﬀerence
SMARTﬁt’s engaging, gamiﬁed training makes it more than just a therapeutic tool—
