What is Brain Health and How Could it Impact You and Your Business?
As science and technology have evolved and we have come to understand the power of the brain 
to enhance both physical and cognitive performance, Brain Health is quickly emerging as the next 
concept in human performance training. As it emerges, it is benefiting all populations, from early 
childhood learning to high-level sports and tactical performance, to preventing falls and slowing 
the progression of dementia and Parkinson’s for older adults. It’s a powerful and timely concept for 
humanity, and it also represents a major business opportunity.
Allow the brain to lead and it will elevate the health and performance of everyone. That’s the 
fundamental premise behind Brain Health training. Everything we do in real life starts with the brain, 
and almost all human activity requires doing a physical and cognitive task at the same time. It’s 
not surprising that recent science has demonstrated that training the brain and body together, not 
separately as we have traditionally done, transfers to improved real-life performance faster and more 
effectively than physical or cognitive training alone. 
There is reason for optimism that the brain is finally getting the respect it is due. 
The 2025 World Economic Forum at Davos, Switzerland listed Brain Health (neurological health, mental 
health, and cognitive performance) as one of their highest priorities in recognition of both its human 
and economic impact. It also mentioned the importance of physical exercise.
Industry professionals who’ve heard about dual-task cognitive-motor training and other brain-led 
solutions are asking how to include Brain Health in their programming. Key markets where we’re seeing 
accelerated growth include physical, occupational, speech, and neuro rehabilitation, YMCAs, senior 
living including assisted living and memory care, longevity programs, community health & recreation 
centers, medical wellness, military bases, athletic programs, fitness facilities, Boys and Girls Clubs,
and education.
Revolutionary new technologies are driving interest and success with unique and effective Brain Health 
solutions, some of which can measure baselines and track progress. SMARTfit initiated this Guide and 
is also actively collaborating with other suppliers featured later in the room designs. 
It’s important, as you learn more about Brain Health to comprehend the distinction between “bottom-up” 
and “top-down” cognitive processing. Bottom-up solutions challenge and train the brain and body to react 
faster.  Top-down solutions engage users in more complex activities, uniquely challenging the executive 
function domain of the brain to not only react faster, but to make quicker and better decisions, improve 
working memory, enhance cognitive flexibility, and more. 
All this happens while performing a variety of physical movements and tasks, which carry over to 
performance in life. Studies have revealed that it is this combination of reaction and executive function 
training that leads to greater transference to real-world results than simpler reaction or non-brain-led

SMARTfit Uses the Science-Based Adaptive Capacity Model to
Deliver Training
As hunter gatherers, humans had to use their brains and bodies together in order to survive and 
advance. Thus, humans evolved by developing the ability to cognitively problem solve, plan, and make 
decisions in order to finally act, using motor skills to achieve the goal. This interaction between brain 
and body produced the human evolution of today and is deeply embedded in our DNA.

Since the start of the industrial revolution and more recently computerization, we have shifted to largely 
inactive, desk-bound, and seated activity. As a result, humans are increasingly sedentary, resulting in a 
myriad of physical and cognitive conditions which drive up health costs, damage quality of life and limit 
our abilities in all walks of life.
Up until now, the fitness, wellness and performance industries have yet to fully embrace this cognitive- 
motor dual task (CMDT) function as a critical component of the solutions they provide. However, the 
physical therapy industry has been using this technique for the past thirty years, producing extensive 
evidence that training the brain and body together (dual tasking) provides faster results than training 
either one alone. Recent studies specific to SMARTfit have confirmed this.

Provides the Capacity to Deliver a Wide Variety of Applications on a

SMARTfit’s Technology includes its own proprietary operating system that drives multiple product lines, 
each designed to serve different application needs. As the illustration explains, the systems deliver content 
from an iOS/Android tablet that is connected to the Cloud. Programming is delivered via 20 game suites 
and 3,000 pre-designed activities that have been developed to serve every demographic group.
How SMARTfit Works
A high-speed controller receives instructions from the SMARTfit Android/iOS App user interface, 
distributing gamified content to and from any number of computerized targets located in front of and 
around the individual. Each target features a computerized display providing stimulus and an adjustable 
touch sensor capturing responses, which trains the participant to manage their power appropriately.
The purpose is to stimulate each participant’s physical and cognitive interaction in a way that simulates 
real life. Progressive leveling enables participants of any age or ability to enhance their cognitive and 
physical abilities at a pace appropriate to themselves.

3.	 SMARTfit MultiTrainer Gen 2 – This system is available with the Education Controller Software

participants per station. It is only available as an on-wall installation for gyms and multi-purpose 
training rooms and is blue in color. Scores are tracked on each station, setting up competition with 
an additional scoreboard that tracks the total score of all stations. Software now offers an extended 
suite of games and pre-designed activities and programs including data tracking of individuals in a 
large group setting.
4.	 SMARTfit ProTrainer Gen 2 – This system differs from the MultiTrainer in that the panels/
stations are joined together. The Performance Controller Software License (required) operates

develop new and more efficient neural pathways.
