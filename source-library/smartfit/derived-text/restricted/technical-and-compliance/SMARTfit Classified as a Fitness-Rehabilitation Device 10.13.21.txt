SMARTfit Classification as a Fitness/Rehabilitation Device 
 
 
 
To whom this may concern: 
 
SMARTfit serves persons without a known medical condition for their own health/wellness and 
preventative care. For this reason, SMARTfit is classified as a fitness/rehabilitation device rather than a 
medical device that diagnoses and treats medical conditions. As a result, it has not been necessary to 
seek FDA classification.  This is supported by the following:   
 
1. There are no moving parts or components that are attached to any part of the person. 
2. The system functions purely to engage the person in cognitive and motor activities that provide 
the opportunity of repeated practice with the aim to improve cognitive and motor performance. 
 
SMARTfit activities and programs rely on the trainer/therapist to decide which of them is appropriate 
and suitable for their clients. As with a fitness treadmill or weight machine, a trainer/therapist needs to 
know whether the equipment, its user options, or programs are suitable for the client before 
use.  Further, knowing the client’s capability will be essential to how the equipment is used. 
  
SMARTfit is a non-invasive fitness/rehabilitation device that is not going to put the person at risk as a 
treadmill or weight machine would.  That said, the trainer still needs to know whether the client should 
be doing seated only programs, standing programs, or moving programs.  If the user is engaging in 
movement, the trainer also needs to be aware of safety issues relating to the use of accessory 
equipment used during the activities.  For example, they may decide not to put a user on a Bosu ball to 
practice balance while playing on the SMARTfit system without the supervision of a trainer/therapist. 
 
In summary, SMARTfit incorporates programming and activities for all levels of ability, but the 
trainer/therapist must assess their client and decide which ones are a suitable match for the client.  
These are considered standard requirements of fitness/rehabilitation devices rather than medical 
devices.  Since SMARTfit is NOT a medical device, its intended use and indications for use do NOT 
include diagnosis or treatment.  Rather, it is a tool for the trainer/therapist to use to train their 
clients.  For this reason, there is no need for risk assessment as you would normally find with a medical 
device.
