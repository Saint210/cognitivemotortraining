## Core positioning proposed in the dataset

The submitted brief positions SMARTfit around combining movement with cognitive tasks in gamified dual-task training. It proposes benefits involving cognitive function, motor skills, mobility, rehabilitation, recovery, cognitive decline, and human performance.

These are marketing propositions, not approved claims. Claims about improved function, faster outcomes, neural pathways, recovery, prevention, decline, or transfer to daily activities require precise and suitable evidence, review, and permission before publication.

## Differentiators proposed in the dataset

- Dual-task training and a claimed research history of more than 30 years.
- Gamified, high-repetition target interaction and proposed effects on engagement, neuroplasticity, and executive function.
- Named assessments, including Stroop, Trail Making A/B, Digit Span, Corsi Block, Serial 7s, and Dual Task Cost.
- Proposed objective baseline and progress data.
- Business outcomes including patient acquisition and retention, CPT-coded and cash-based revenue, and reduced therapist burnout.
- Setup, QR wristband login, multi-site dashboards, and over-the-air updates.

### Review cautions

- Research duration or quantity does not by itself establish the effectiveness of a specific SMARTfit product.
- Confirm each assessment's exact name, availability, license, model compatibility, validation, intended use, and any relevant permissions.
- Revenue, reimbursement/CPT, acquisition, retention, and staffing claims require specific substantiation and legal/compliance review.
- Verify setup time, login workflow, dashboard availability, update process, and data handling against current documentation.

## Audiences and use cases proposed in the dataset

- Rehabilitation and physical therapy, including PT, OT, and SLP; orthopedic, neurological, vestibular, pediatric, and other therapy settings.
- Senior living and active aging, including independent living, assisted living, memory care, and skilled nursing.
- Sports performance and tactical settings, including athletes, military, police, and fire personnel.
- Education and youth, including physical education, special education, and early childhood programs.

This list can inform audience research and site planning. Confirm the current product fit and appropriate language for each setting. Conditions, diagnoses, fall-risk reduction, dementia mitigation, sensory integration, and other health or developmental outcomes must not be presented as established SMARTfit benefits without claim-specific evidence and approval.

## Proposed copy requiring evidence review

The submitted dataset proposes these headline and positioning ideas:

- "Train Brain and Body Together. Accelerate Real-World Performance."
- "Evidence-based dual-task technology combining physical movement and cognitive processing to restore mobility, prevent decline, and elevate human performance."

Do not use these as approved public copy. "Evidence-based," "accelerate," "restore mobility," and "prevent decline" need precise substantiation and potentially regulatory/legal review. A safer draft should describe verified product capabilities without promising outcomes.
