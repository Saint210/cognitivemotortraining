## Positioning and outcome statements supplied

The source describes SMARTfit as gamified rehabilitation, physical-training, and assessment technology that combines physical movement with cognitive tasks. It proposes benefits concerning executive function, cognitive and physical performance, mobility, balance, coordination, decision-making, reaction, activities of daily living, independence, quality of life, fall risk, and cognitive decline.

It also proposes that SMARTfit can create or repair neural pathways; improve or restore function; mitigate falls, dementia, or cognitive decline; produce faster results; maximize neuroplasticity; and transfer gains to untrained, real-world activities.

These are high-risk efficacy, clinical, prevention, causality, or generalization claims. They are not approved. Review the exact product, population, outcome, study design, comparison, and result before considering any narrower claim. General dual-task research does not by itself substantiate a SMARTfit-specific outcome.
