## Use guardrails

1. Do not describe the first 18 months as “free service” without distinguishing the product purchase, annual software license, hardware-upgrade exclusion, and on-site visit charges.
2. Keep the two rows distinct: shipping parts to/from the factory is listed as covered in the first two plans and not covered in the third; on-site visits are separately listed at $800 per day plus travel for all three.
3. Do not present the $800, $49, or $100 amounts publicly. They are date-sensitive commercial terms and public pricing is intentionally private.
4. Do not imply the plan includes all hardware or all repairs. The PDF specifically labels the row “Hardware Upgrades” and lists “Not included / Priced upon release”; component coverage is described separately.
5. Do not state current warranty coverage, eligibility, exclusions, response times, service territories, or plan availability based on this 2024-metadata PDF.
6. Do not promise that Robert provides SMARTfit support, training, shipping, installation, or repair services. Provider, territory, and delivery responsibility remain unconfirmed.
7. Annual-license wording concerns updates/upgrades, with an exception for essential maintenance and bug fixes; it does not establish current license names, feature access, or terms.
8. Confirm current terms, contact details, and any revised plan with SMARTfit before quoting a plan or publishing service/warranty information.
