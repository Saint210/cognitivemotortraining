# Positioning and audience map

Status: working strategy, not approved public-facing copy

## Strategic direction

Build one credible SMARTfit information and sales website for the full range of institutional markets the manufacturer serves. The homepage should help visitors identify their setting and route into useful, specific information. It should not imply that one audience is the only or default market.

The site represents Robert Aguilar's SMARTfit sales activity, not SMARTfit, Inc. itself. Robert confirms that SMARTfit products are currently available for sale, Texas is his territory, and he can make sales outside Texas. Use that distinction accurately in public copy; do not imply exclusivity or a broader service footprint. Authorized-dealer status and brand permissions are still unconfirmed. Do not call the business an authorized dealer, distributor, manufacturer, clinic, or global authority without documented approval.

Robert's longer-term business vision is to generate online SMARTfit sales and use revenue toward opening a combination facility in or near San Antonio, Texas. The concept includes product demos, training/wellness, possible qualified clinical services, and evaluation of a separate Hypergravity suit. This is a goal, not an existing location, service, partnership, proven combined protocol, or confirmed financial plan. See [business-vision-and-phases.md](./business-vision-and-phases.md) for phase gates and open decisions.

### Buyer versus participant

The buyer is typically an organization or program leader. The person using the equipment may be a patient, resident, student, athlete, service member, or community participant. Speak to organizational decisions—space, staffing, configuration, intended activity, procurement, and support—while describing end users respectfully and without medicalizing every audience.

## Brand character and voice

- **Professional:** plain, composed, specific, and useful.
- **Evidence-aware:** citations and caveats are visible where claims need them.
- **Consultative:** help buyers decide whether a system fits; do not pressure or imply every visitor needs one.
- **Inclusive across markets:** one visual and editorial system, with audience-specific examples.
- **Human:** use Robert's real credentials and background only as confirmed; do not imply a clinic is open or that he currently works for a former employer.

Avoid hype phrases such as "revolutionary," "industry-leading," "proven to rewire the brain," "guaranteed results," "the best," or unsupported "world's largest" language.

## Primary conversion model

The initial conversion should be a private online consultation or sales inquiry; a current demo request may be offered only where an authorized demo is actually available. A future physical demonstration location is an aspiration, not a current CTA or service. Public prices are intentionally excluded. The process should:

1. Ask for organization, role, setting, location, and primary goals without collecting patient or other sensitive health information.
2. Accept sales inquiries from Texas and beyond; confirm availability and any delivery, installation, or support arrangements before making commitments.
3. Avoid guaranteeing a response time, local service, installation, clinical advice, or dealer assignment until operational details are confirmed.
4. Keep commercial pricing and commission information out of public pages, metadata, analytics events, and browser-delivered application code.
