# SMARTfit product specification review

Status: internal verification workpaper; not approved public copy

## Scope and method

This pass reviewed the page-marked text for all 16 PDFs in `source-library/smartfit/product-specifications/current/2026-07/`. The folder name is an inventory grouping, not a guarantee of document revision: several PDFs have a footer revision of May 2026, Trainer sheets mix 2023/2026 page revisions, and the wired rehab sheet carries a February 2025 revision.

The material is manufacturer-provided according to Robert. This workpaper records what the supplied documents state; it does not establish inventory, resolve every product/license contradiction, or establish efficacy. Dimensions, illustrations, and any values affected by questionable text extraction must be checked visually in the original PDF before publication. A current supplied US reseller catalog/price list effective 1 July 2026 now supplements the PDF and webpage review. It adds current-listed SKUs and configuration-specific license statements; it is not proof of inventory or an on-the-day order quote. Reseller prices are restricted and are not reproduced here.

- **ProTrainer:** The current [ProTrainer page](https://smartfitinc.com/fitness/smartfit-pro-trainer/) identifies **ProTrainer Gen 2**, lists two-, three-, and four-section configurations, and states that On-Wall and On-Frame configurations are available with a minimum one-year P license. This resolves the earlier absence of an on-frame sheet in the local set as a statement about the current web catalog, not as confirmation of a specific on-frame sheet or orderable SKU.
- **Wireless Strike Pods:** The current [Strike Pods page](https://smartfitinc.com/fitness/smartfit-strike-pods/) is headed **Gen 3 Strike Pod Systems – Shipping August 2026** and lists 3-, 5-, 7-, and 9-pod systems. The page says the 3- and 5-pod controllers support up to 9 pods and the 7- and 9-pod controllers support up to 15, with the SP lifetime license included. This reconciles the controller-capacity difference for the current Gen 3 page. The 2026-07 local sheets describe Gen 2; do not combine the generations or assume their controller compatibility and accessories are interchangeable. Because the live page still carries a “Shipping August 2026” heading, it does not by itself establish actual October orderability.
- **License terminology / MultiTrainer:** The current [license options page](https://smartfitinc.com/smartfit-mini-software-license-options/) describes G, P, R, RA, and SP but does not list E. SMARTfit's July 2026 US reseller list now confirms Model E is offered in that list. Its individual MultiTrainer product lines require E for MultiTrainer 2 and P for MultiTrainer 3/4; this matches the official [MultiTrainer WordPress record (page ID 19587)](https://smartfitinc.com/wp-json/wp/v2/pages/19587), marked published and modified 5 October 2026, and the May 2026 Performance License webinar's support for P use. However, the same reseller list's general Model G and Model P descriptions say those licenses are for panel systems “except MultiTrainers,” while its Model E description says E applies to all panel systems except ProTrainers. Thus the exact configuration-specific lines conflict with the general eligibility text for MultiTrainer 3/4. The list also refers to Model E as pre-college education, while the product-page record retains dated 2023 Gen 2 shipping wording; current E/P compatibility, entitlements, and eligibility need written manufacturer confirmation. A direct automated fetch of the [MultiTrainer product URL](https://smartfitinc.com/fitness/smartfit-multi-station/) returned HTTP 410, but Robert confirmed it loaded in his browser and the page content was retrievable through a cache-busted URL. Treat the page as accessible; a direct-fetch response is not evidence that the page is unavailable. Do not publish a definitive license recommendation until SMARTfit resolves the catalog's internal contradiction.
- **Wired Strike Pods:** The current [Wired Strike Pods page](https://smartfitinc.com/fitness/strike-pods-wired/) identifies the systems as Gen 2 and describes multiple target-count configurations and different game/test availability by target count. However, the page's “Wired 5 Strike Pod System” hardware description says “3 Strike Pods,” an apparent internal inconsistency. Do not copy the 5-kit contents or generalize game entitlements until SMARTfit confirms the current specifications.
- **Wired 5-pod kit:** The July 2026 reseller list also labels its Wired 5 Strike Pod system as a 5-pod item but describes the included hardware as 3 Strike Pods. This repeats the product-page count discrepancy; confirm kit contents before quoting.
- **Numeric, revision, and availability limits:** The current web information does not visually validate suspect dimensions or conversions in the PDFs, reconcile mixed revision footers, or establish stock/orderability of every SKU listed in the reseller catalog. Preserve the visual-PDF and model-specific quote confirmation gate below.

## Current spec-file findings

### Mini systems

- **High-impact Mini:** the current-dated sheets describe one 46 x 46 inch interactive panel with nine computerized targets. The wall and frame versions have adjustable height up to approximately seven feet; the frame/portable versions differ in their stand or mobility hardware. The specifications list G, P, R, or RA annual license options, require a tablet supplied by the customer, and describe accessories including drumsticks, foam noodles, a two-pound bouncing medicine ball, and a hurdle (performance) or stability pad (rehab). Sources: [High Impact Mini On-Frame, PDF pp. 1-2](../source-library/smartfit/product-specifications/current/2026-07/SMARTfit%20High%20Impact%20Mini%20on%20Frame%202026-07.pdf), [High Impact Mini On-Wall, PDF pp. 1-2](../source-library/smartfit/product-specifications/current/2026-07/SMARTfit%20High%20Impact%20Mini%20on%20Wall%202026-07.pdf), [High Impact Mini Portable, PDF pp. 1-2](../source-library/smartfit/product-specifications/current/2026-07/SMARTfit%20High%20Impact%20Mini%20Portable%202026-07.pdf).
- **Low-impact Mini:** the wall and portable sheets describe a 36 x 36 inch panel with nine targets and adjustable height from 18 inches to six feet. The portable version is mounted on a wheeled frame. Both describe the tablet as customer-supplied and a minimum one-year G, P, R, or RA license. The low-impact wall sheet lists drumsticks and a stability pad; the portable sheet lists drumsticks. The wall sheet names seated/standing use contexts including neuro-rehab, long-term care, assisted living, skilled nursing, and early-childhood settings. These are source-stated target contexts, not outcome evidence. Sources: [Low Impact Mini On-Wall, PDF pp. 1-2](../source-library/smartfit/product-specifications/current/2026-07/SMARTfit%20Low%20Impact%20Mini%20on%20Wall%202026-07.pdf), [Low Impact Mini Portable, PDF pp. 1-2](../source-library/smartfit/product-specifications/current/2026-07/SMARTfit%20Low%20Impact%20Mini%20Portable%202026-07.pdf).
- **Numeric caution:** OCR-extracted dimensions and weights on some Mini sheets appear internally inconsistent (for example, metric/imperial conversions or labels do not align). Do not publish dimensions, weights, or installation clearances from extracted text until each original page is visually checked.
